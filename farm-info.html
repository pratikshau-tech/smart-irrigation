Farm Dashboard

The farm dashboard gives basic information about the irrigation process, sensors and different stages of sugarcane growth.

Irrigation Workflow

  1. Collect data from farm sensors.
  2. Check soil moisture and weather conditions.
  3. Analyse the collected information.
  4. Prepare an irrigation recommendation.
  5. Provide the recommendation to the farmer.

Sensors Used

  • Soil Moisture Sensor
  • Temperature Sensor
  • Humidity Sensor
  • Rainfall Sensor

Crop Stages

Germination
Initial stage when the crop starts growing.
Tillering
Stage when new shoots develop from the plant.
Grand Growth
Main growth stage when the crop develops rapidly.
Maturity
Final stage when the crop becomes ready for harvesting.

Farm Monitoring

Regular monitoring of the farm helps in understanding soil and weather conditions before irrigation is carried out.

Smart irrigation helps in using water carefully and supporting healthy crop growth.
